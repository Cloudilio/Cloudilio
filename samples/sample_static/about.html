About us

Harbor Coffee started as a cart on the quay in 2019. Today it's a small room with eight tables, a roaster we share with two other cafés, and a baker who starts at four.

We pour single-origin espresso, a house filter that changes every week and loose-leaf teas. Bring your own cup and the coffee is on us every tenth time.
